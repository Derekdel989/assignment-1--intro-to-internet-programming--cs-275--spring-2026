Ten Principles of Design

By: Dieter Rams

The man, Dieter Rams

Dieter Rams was born in Wiesbaden, Germany in 1932. He was an influencial industrial designer that inspired a large amount of products. Dieter was one of the first designers that strived for environmentally friendly products before it became popular. During the 1970's, Dieter Rams created the Ten Principles of Good Design. He felt that these principles were some of the most important to follow:

  • Innovative
  • Usefulness
  • Aesthetic
  • Understandable
  • Unobtrusive
  • Honest
  • Long-Lasting
  • Thorough to the Last Detail
  • Environmentally Friendly
  • Little Design as Possible
